# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme manages projects through a lifecycle that validates ideas, turns approved work into actionable plans, delivers value iteratively, releases safely, and captures improvements. Initiation confirms the business need, stakeholders, success measures, timeline, risks, and resources in a project one-pager and determines whether work should proceed. Planning then creates a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, release plans, and risk and dependency plans.

During execution, teams use a project board and regular delivery cadence to move work from backlog through review, QA, and done. The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes and prioritizes the backlog; Developers implement and test; QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Delivery emphasizes small, testable increments, automated testing and linting in CI, reviews, metrics, and escalation when blockers grow beyond team-level resolution.

Release activities verify readiness, including completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, rollback or mitigation plans, and smoke tests. Teams then deploy, verify production health, and communicate the release to stakeholders and support. Closeout and retrospectives capture lessons and convert prioritized improvements into backlog or issue actions with owners and due dates. Throughout the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, evidence-based decisions, and psychological safety.

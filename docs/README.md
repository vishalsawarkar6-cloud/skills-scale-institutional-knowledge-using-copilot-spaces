# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project management guidance, from validating a new initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The documents are intended to provide a shared, lightweight way for cross-functional teams to plan work, coordinate execution, manage risk, and learn from outcomes.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme's lifecycle begins with **initiation**, where the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines measurable outcomes, and makes a go/no-go decision. During **planning**, an approved initiative is shaped into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and documented risks and dependencies.

In **execution**, the team tracks work on a project board, coordinates through regular team and stakeholder updates, and uses testing, reviews, metrics, and blocker escalation to keep delivery visible and reliable. **Release** work confirms acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans before deployment and stakeholder communication. After a sprint, release, or milestone, the team **closes and improves** by capturing learnings, holding a retrospective, and tracking a small set of actionable improvements.

The process relies on clear ownership across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders. It emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, evidence-informed decisions, transparent communication, and psychological safety.
